Where the money sits

Contributions are collected into your stokvel's own account, not a black.tax account. black.tax never holds your money. Payments to lenders go out by EFT, and any loan to another stokvel settles in USDC through an escrow on Arc that releases funds only under these rules. Every movement is written to the ledger.
